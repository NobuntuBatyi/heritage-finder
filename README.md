# South African Inheritance Finder System

A secure, AI-powered web application that helps South Africans discover whether they have any unclaimed inheritance, deceased estate benefits, pensions, insurance payouts, trust benefits, or other entitlements — using only their South African ID number.

No account creation is required. The platform is designed to make the process simple, dignified, and respectful, giving ordinary people a way to find what may rightfully belong to them.

## How It Works

1. **Enter your South African ID number** into the secure lookup form.
2. **The system verifies your identity** and checks against participating databases.
3. **Receive clear guidance** on whether any unclaimed benefits or inheritances may be linked to your profile.

## Development Possibilities

This project can grow in several meaningful directions:

- **Direct integrations** with the Master of the High Court, Government Pensions Administration Agency (GPAA), insurance providers, and pension administrators.
- **AI-driven matching engine** to connect potential beneficiaries with deceased estates and unclaimed funds across multiple data sources.
- **POPIA-compliant verification** via OTP, digital signature, or trusted third-party identity services.
- **Mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA)** so users can check their status from any device.
- **SMS and email notifications** to alert users when new potential matches are found.
- **Multi-language support** to reach South Africans in their preferred language.
- **Partner portal** for authorised institutions to register, submit, and manage beneficiary data securely.
- **Fraud detection and audit logging** to keep the system secure and trustworthy.

## A Note From the Creator

This app was created by **Nobuntu Batyi**.

The idea came from a deeply personal place: watching families lose what should have been passed down to them simply because they did not know where to look, who to ask, or how to begin. Too many South Africans remain unaware of inheritances, pensions, and benefits that were meant to support them — and the people they love — through difficult times.

This platform exists to change that. It is not just about data or matching records; it is about restoring dignity, reconnecting families with what is rightfully theirs, and giving every South African the chance to say: *"I know, and I can act."*

If this app helps even one person find peace, closure, or a long-overdue benefit, then it has served its purpose.

— Nobuntu Batyi
